Image source & what is simulated

Image: LifeAct-EGFP actin in a COS-7 cell, Mo et al. (2023), Figure 1b. Green-channel crop from the published BF-SIM image, resampled to 256 × 256 pixels. Read the paper ↗ · CC BY 4.0.

Acquisition: the reference is multiplied by nine sinusoidal patterns (three phases at three approximately 60°-spaced orientations), then passed through an idealized circular low-pass detection model. These are synthetic frames, not experimental raw data. Frame timing is for presentation.

Reconstruction: phase demodulation, known integer-bin frequency shifts and weighted band combination are computed from the synthetic frames. The same detection model produces the widefield comparison. No noise, motion, bleaching or aberrations are added. The finite reference image limits the available detail; this demonstration makes no calibrated resolution claim. Display scaling is linear and fixed across compared images; camera-frame scaling is shared across all nine frames.

Optics: ideal binary phase grating and two plane waves. Geometry is schematic. This is two-beam 2D-SIM; 3D-SIM needs a different illumination and acquisition scheme.

The real nine-frame acquisition and matching reconstruction can replace this example once available.
